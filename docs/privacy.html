Tempo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Tempo is a phone app and a Wear OS watch app that lets you control the media playing on your Android phone from your watch. This policy explains what information the apps use and what happens to it. In short: Tempo does not collect your data, and nothing is sent to the developer or to any server run by the developer.

Who we are

Tempo is developed by Ajinkya (github.com/ajudi46). For questions about this policy, contact ajudi46@gmail.com.

What Tempo accesses

To show and control what is playing, the phone app reads the following from your phone:

  • The name of the app that is playing media (for example, your music app).
  • The title, artist and artwork of the current track or episode.
  • Playback state (playing or paused), the position and length of the track, and the phone's media volume.

The watch app sends your button presses (play or pause, next, previous) and crown volume changes back to the phone so the phone can carry them out.

Notification access

Android only lets an app see and control another app's media session if it has been granted notification access. Tempo asks for this permission for that single purpose. Although the permission technically allows an app to read notifications, Tempo does not read, store or transmit the content of your notifications. You can turn this permission off at any time in your phone's Settings, which stops Tempo from working.

How information moves between your devices

The information above is sent from your phone to your paired watch, and commands are sent back, using the Wearable Data Layer provided by Google Play services. This communication happens directly between your own devices (for example over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi) and is handled by Google Play services as part of Wear OS. Google's own privacy policy applies to that service.

What we collect and share

  • We do not collect, store on any server, sell, rent or share your personal information.
  • Tempo has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
  • Tempo does not use your location, contacts, microphone, camera or files.

Data stored on your devices

The most recent track details and artwork are kept on your phone and watch by Wear OS so the watch can display them. They are replaced when the track changes and are removed when you uninstall the apps. Tempo does not keep a history of what you have listened to.

Children

Tempo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
